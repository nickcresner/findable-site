FAQ: fair questions

What does it cost to start a monthly plan?

Start with the £129 Profile Fix-Up as a one-off setup, then £59 a month for Findable Monthly or £99 a month for Findable Plus. Your first month is free. Rolling monthly, cancel any time.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile and can remove us any time.

Can you get me to #1 on Google or guarantee ChatGPT mentions me?

No – and be wary of anyone who says they can. We improve the signals Google and AI assistants use, and show you the trend each month.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. Only real customers, no incentives. It's against Google's rules and UK law.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You get a WhatsApp or text when something needs your thumbs-up. That's it.

Where do you cover?

Starting with N2, N3, N12, EN4, EN5 and NW7, and the rest of North London.
