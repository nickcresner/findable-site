FAQ: fair questions

What does it cost to start a monthly plan?

Nothing today. Findable Essentials (visibility) and Findable Pro (visibility plus missed-call text-back, appointment reminders with reply YES or R to reschedule, and review requests) both start with your first month free (14 days), then £59 or £99 a month. Most people start with the £129 Profile Fix-Up, a one-off that sorts the profile first; the monthly plans keep it right. Rolling monthly, cancel any time.

What does Findable Pro do that Essentials doesn't?

Pro adds the admin: if you miss a call, the caller gets a text straight away with a link to book a call-back. Customers get a booking confirmation plus reminders the day before and on the morning, and can reply YES to confirm or R to reschedule. After every job, each customer gets a thank-you and your Google review link (no incentives, no cherry-picking).

How do I know this is genuine and not a scam?

Good question to ask. I'm not Google and never say I am, and your Google profile is free. I'll never ask for your password or a verification code, never ask for payment over the phone, and never say your listing will be removed. You stay the owner: if you want help, you add me as a Manager yourself and can remove me any time. You can check who I am on LinkedIn and at nickcresner.com before you reply.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile and can remove us any time.

Can you get me to #1 on Google or guarantee ChatGPT mentions me?

No – and be wary of anyone who says they can. We improve the signals Google and AI assistants use, and show you the trend each month.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. Only real customers, no incentives. It's against Google's rules and UK law.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You get a WhatsApp or text when something needs your thumbs-up. That's it.

Is this like Checkatrade, Bark or MyBuilder?

No. We don't sell leads, so there are no lead fees, credits or shortlist charges, and nobody else is sent your customer. We improve your own free Google Business Profile so people who search for a plumber near them find you and call you directly. Plenty of trades keep a directory listing as well, and that's fine.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, on the £129 Profile Fix-Up (setup) fee. If you're not happy, email hello@findable.london within 30 days of paying and we refund the £129 in full, no questions asked. Just tell us by email; you don't need to give a reason.

Is there a contract? How do I cancel?

No contract and no minimum term. The monthly plans roll month to month. To cancel, email hello@findable.london and we stop the subscription and remove our Manager access to your profile.

If the first month is free, when do I pay?

Monthly plans take nothing at checkout. Your first payment, £59 for Essentials or £99 for Pro, is taken 14 days after you start; cancel before then and you pay nothing. The Profile Fix-Up is a separate one-off £129, paid at checkout, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

What happens to my Google profile if I stop?

It stays yours. Your profile, photos, posts and reviews belong to your business. We were only ever a Manager, and we remove ourselves when you leave.

What actually helps a plumber show up on Google Maps?

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Distance is out of anyone's hands, so we work on the rest: the right main category, your services and areas written out, correct hours, real photos, and a steady flow of genuine reviews that you reply to.

Do I need a website?

No. Most of the work happens on your Google profile. If you do have a website, we make sure its name, address and phone match your profile, because Google and AI assistants cross-check them.

Where do you cover?

Starting with N2, N3, N12, EN4, EN5 and NW7, then the rest of North London and Hertfordshire.
